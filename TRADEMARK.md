# Trademark policy

HarmonyGo is an MIT-licensed project. **The license covers the code; it does not
grant the project name or logo.**

## What you may do

- Fork the code, modify it, and distribute it under MIT.
- Use the name **HarmonyGo** to refer to the upstream project factually, e.g.
  “a fork of HarmonyGo” or “compatible with HarmonyGo”.
- Link to the official repository and releases.

## What you may not do

- Ship a modified build under the name **HarmonyGo** or with the official
  lightning mark / logo, or in a way that suggests it is an official release.
- Use the name or mark in an app title, store listing, domain, or product name
  that could be confused with the official project.
- Remove or alter copyright and license notices in the source.

If you distribute a modified version, **rebrand it** (name, icon, bundle id) and
make clear that it is not endorsed by the upstream maintainers. You are welcome
to say it is “based on HarmonyGo”.

## Official releases

Official releases are published only from:

- <https://github.com/QtePP/harmony-go>
- <https://atomgit.com/qtepp/harmony-go>

A build downloaded anywhere else is not an official release.

## Contact

Open an issue in the official repository for permission requests or questions.
This policy is not legal advice; it is the project's good-faith trademark rule.
